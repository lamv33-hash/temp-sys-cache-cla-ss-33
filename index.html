        How to run well Form and habits

        1. Run slower than you think. You should be able to speak in full sentences. If not, walk until you can.
        2. Take quick, short steps. Aim for about 165–180 steps per minute and avoid reaching out with your front foot.
        3. Stand tall. Lean slightly from the ankles, keep your eyes 10–20 meters ahead and your shoulders low.
        4. Land under your hips. Keep your knees soft. The part of the foot that lands matters less than where it lands.
        5. Keep arms loose. Bend your elbows near 90°, swing front to back and keep your hands relaxed.
        6. Breathe from your belly. Breathe through your nose and mouth. Gasping means it’s time for a walk break.

        Run three days a week with a rest day between. Keep weekly distance growth under about 10%, and ease off if you feel sharp pain.
